# Internal Directories

Who to call, where things are, and who's on call. All of it lives in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder), so it stays behind your Southland login.

## What you'll find there

| Directory | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Internal Support Directory | Stake bed requests, controls jobs, PO questions, vendor account holds, WBS codes, warehouse pickup verification, timecards, overhead accounting codes, direct deposit, and office supplies |
| Account setup routing | Brand new customer and vendor accounts in COINS and ServiceTitan |
| Escalation contacts | Who to call when the Field Supervisor isn't available |
| Technician roster | Names, roles, specialties, and union locals |
| On-call schedule | This week's on-call technicians and phone numbers |
| Internal locations | Warehouse and will-call addresses, plus the PCard billing address |

> Tip: Contacts are listed in the order to try them. If the first person doesn't answer, move to the next.
