## What's in the Onboarding Folder

These live in SharePoint because they include names, phone numbers, account numbers, or internal codes:

- Internal Support Directory: who to contact for POs, timecards, vendor holds, WBS codes, and office supplies
- Escalation contacts, technician roster, and the on-call schedule
- Internal locations, including the warehouse and PCard billing address
- Customer and vendor setup mailboxes
- Cost codes, overhead codes, and PO approval limits
- PAMF pharmacy sites, contacts, and scheduling windows
- Customer acronyms and nicknames
- The dispatcher weekly timesheet and the Service Request Intake Form

## How to add a link

1. In SharePoint, open the file or folder and choose Share, then Copy link.
2. Set it to People in Southland Industries.
3. Add a new line to the list above, like this: `- [PM Material Lists](paste-the-link-here)`
4. In any article, link to it by its name in lowercase with dashes: `[PM Material Lists](pm-material-lists)`

> Important: Always copy links with Share, then Copy link. Links copied from the browser address bar break when a file or folder is renamed.
