# Email Templates

Copy, paste, and fill in the brackets. Every template has a Copy button in the corner.

## Message received

> Your service request has been received, and we're working on assigning a technician.
> We'll reach out as soon as it's scheduled.
> Thank you for your patience.

## New customer

> We appreciate you choosing Southland Industries to service your business.
> To set you up in our system, please fill out the attached form and return it with your most recent signed and dated W9.
> Feel free to reach out with any questions. Thank you.

## New vendor setup in COINS

Send to the Accounts Payable mailbox listed in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).

> Can we please get this new vendor set up in COINS?
> Here is their new vendor form and W9.

## New vendor setup in ServiceTitan

For vendors that already exist in COINS. Send to the ServiceTitan vendor setup contacts in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).

> Can we please get this vendor set up in ServiceTitan?
> Vendor Name: [Vendor Name]
> COINS Account Number: [Account Number]
> Address: [Street, City, State ZIP]
> Let me know if you need anything else. Thanks!

## New location setup

For existing customers only. Send to the Accounts Receivable mailbox.

> Can we please get a new location set up?
> Location Name: [Customer] [City] [Street Number]
> Address: [Street, City, State ZIP]
> Customer Account Number: [Account Number]
> A screenshot of the account is attached. Let us know if you need any other information.
> Thank you,

## New job

> Hello,
> Job [Job Number] has been created for this quoted job and placed on [Technician]'s unscheduled board.
> [Sales Rep], please let us know what POs you may need for this job.
> Please let us know if anyone needs anything else.

## New construction job

See [Technician Notes for Construction](technician-notes-for-construction) for when to use this.

> Please reply to this email with any job notes and updates so I can add them to the job.
> Please use the following labor cost code: [Labor Transfer Cost Code]
> Please include this in your payroll job description line: Job [Job Number] - [Customer] [Customer PO]

## New PO

> Subject: PO [PO Number] for [Vendor Name], Job [Job Number]
> Please use PO [PO Number] for this request.
> Please send the receipt when available.
> Let me know if I can help with anything else.

## Part request

> Subject: Part Request, Job [Job Number]
> Can you please help me place an order for the attached quote?
> Please use PO [PO Number].
> Please ship to: [Address]
> ATTN: [Lead Technician]
> Let me know if you need anything else. Thanks so much!

## PM scheduling

> Good afternoon,
> We're reaching out to schedule your upcoming preventive maintenance service at [Address]. Will these dates work for your team?
> First visit: [Day, Date], start time [Time], technician [Name]
> Second visit: [Day, Date], start time [Time], technician [Name]
> Please let us know if a different day or time works better for you.

## On call list

Send Friday morning. The dates in the subject match the dates for "the week of."

> Subject: On Call List for the Week of [Mon Date] - [Mon Date]
> Hey team,
> On call technician for this weekend: [Fri Date] - [Mon Date], [Technician], [Phone]
> On call technician for the week of: [Mon Date] - [Mon Date], [Technician], [Phone]
> Have a great weekend, and stay safe out there!

> Note: "This weekend" runs Friday to Monday. "The week of" runs Monday to Monday. The on-call schedule is in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
