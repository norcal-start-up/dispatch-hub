# PCard Purchases
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When parts, tools, or training are bought with the company credit card.

## Steps

1. Use the PCard billing address for every purchase. It's in the Internal Directories in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
2. On the job, scroll down to History and click Files.
3. Scroll to PCard Receipt Upload. Hover and click Create.
4. Add the technician's name, the receipt image, the vendor name, and the amount.
5. Confirm the charge pulled into the job's Materials.

![Create a PCard Receipt Upload, then confirm it pulled into Materials. The technician's name is blurred.](pcard-purchases-01.png)

## Required Information

- Receipt
