# Creating Quoted Jobs
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When Sales emails you an opportunity link for a quote the customer approved.

## Steps

### Check the estimate

1. Open the estimate link.
2. Confirm the status is Sold.
3. Click Edit, the pencil in the top right.
4. Under Price Details, confirm the Rate Sheet shows No Rate Sheet.

![Confirm the estimate shows Sold](creating-quoted-jobs-01.png)
![The rate sheet should read No Rate Sheet](creating-quoted-jobs-02.png)

### Prepare the estimate for import

1. Delete each estimate item. Click the three dots next to each line and choose Delete the item. The estimate should show $0.00 when you're done.
2. Click View All under documents and download the estimate file.
3. Close the Forms, Media, and Attachments window.

### Import the estimate

1. Go to Actions, then Import from Template, and select the file you downloaded.
2. Wait for the import, then verify the total cost and price match the estimate. The price total includes tax. The subtotal doesn't.
3. Minimize the cost group with the down arrow.
4. Check the boxes for everything except the cost section you minimized.
5. Click Add to Job to add the pricing.

### Book the job

1. Click Book New Job and enter the job details:
   - Job Type: Quoted
   - Business Unit: Norcal Bay Area - HVAC
   - Marketing Campaign: Existing Customer
   - Commission Percentage: set by Sales. 1% means the technician helped with some of the quoting. 2% means they handled most of it.
2. Click Book Job. You'll return to the estimate screen.
3. Go to Actions, then Add to Job. Select your new job number and click Submit.
4. Under Created Jobs, click the job number.
5. Click the paperclip and upload the setup documents: the request email, photos, vendor quotes, the estimate, and the proposal.

![Job details for a quoted job. Names and the customer PO are blurred.](creating-quoted-jobs-03.png)
![Upload documents with the paperclip under History](creating-quoted-jobs-04.png)

> Tip: Open the job's invoice, go to Job Costing, and check the margin. If the quote went in correctly with no estimated cost, the margin shows 100%.

## Common Errors

- Creating the job before the customer approves the quote. Wait for Sold.
- Leaving the estimate items in place before importing, which doubles the price.
