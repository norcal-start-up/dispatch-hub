# Receiving Purchase Orders
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When the invoice comes in. A PO has to be received before the job can bill.

## Steps

1. Open the PO and click Receive.
2. Under Receipt Attachments, click Upload File and attach the invoice.
3. Type in the Vendor Document Number from the invoice.
4. Update the Unit Cost with the pre-tax cost.
5. Update Shipping Cost with any freight charges.

![Find the PO, click Receive, then update Unit Cost and Shipping Cost](receiving-purchase-orders-01.png)

## Required Information

- PO number
- Invoice
- Pre-tax costs
- Shipping costs
