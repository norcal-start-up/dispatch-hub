# Material or Subcontractor PO?
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When you can't tell whether a PO request is for materials or a subcontractor.

## Steps

1. Look at the words in the request and its attachments. These usually mean subcontractor work:
   - Inspecting
   - Repair work
   - Engineering services
   - Testing and balancing
2. Open the email attachments.
3. Look for clues in the vendor's quote.
4. If you're still not sure, ask the requestor.
