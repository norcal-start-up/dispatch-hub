# Customer Communication Standards
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Southland's standards for every customer email and call.

## Steps

1. Respond to customer email requests within 15 minutes, and let them know you've received the request.
2. Confirm what time the technician can arrive, so there are no surprises.
3. Send a confirmation email with the technician's name, the date, and the agreed time.
4. After a Preventive Maintenance visit, email the service report.

## Required Information

- Site contact name
- Technician name
- Agreed date and time of the visit
