# Job Completion Checklist
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Before you tag a job Ready to Review. Every box here should be true.

## Job details

- [ ] Customer PO is filled in correctly.
- [ ] Requested By has the customer's first and last name.
- [ ] Salesperson and Supervisor are correct.
- [ ] Every completed appointment is marked Complete.
- [ ] Unused appointments are deleted.
- [ ] All required forms are complete.
- [ ] Totals match the PCard Receipt Uploads under Files and the Invoice Details lines.
- [ ] No Field Supervisor questions or corrections are left open under History.

## Invoice details

- [ ] Technician notes in the Invoice Summary say the job is complete.
- [ ] Every PO is marked Received, with an invoice.
- [ ] The right rate sheet is selected. Quoted jobs use No Rate Sheets. T&M jobs use NorCal Contract or NorCal Non-Contract.
- [ ] Tax Zone is set to CAEX.
- [ ] The Service Vehicle Fee is applied, on T&M jobs only.

## Job costing

- [ ] The job has a subtotal.
- [ ] Margin isn't negative on the Job Costing tab.
- [ ] All technician hours have pulled over from timesheets.

> Note: Your ticks are saved in this browser. Click Clear all, next to the progress bar, before you start the next job.
