# Creating T&M Jobs
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a customer has a problem and agrees to pay for time and materials.

## Steps

1. Go to Calls and choose Manual job.
2. Search for the service location by name or street. Select the location with the Job Site tag.
3. Enter the job details:
   - Job Type: Time and Material
   - Business Unit: Norcal Bay Area - HVAC
   - Marketing Campaign: Existing Customer
   - Requested By: the customer's first and last name
   - Customer PO: the PO, or the customer's name and "NEED PO" until you have one
4. Click Book Job. You'll land on the new job screen.
5. Scroll down to History and click the paperclip. Upload the setup documents, such as a PDF of the request email and any photos.

## Required Information

- Service location
- Requestor name
- Customer PO or placeholder
- Scope of work

## Common Errors

- Picking a location tagged Bill To Only. Work can only be scheduled at a Job Site location.
- Leaving Customer PO blank. Jobs can't bill without it.
