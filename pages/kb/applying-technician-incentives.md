# Applying Technician Incentives
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a technician finds new work during an active visit and asks for the incentive.

## Steps

1. When setting up the job, open the Commission Percentage dropdown.
2. Select $T&M.

![Select $T&M from Commission Percentage](applying-technician-incentives-01.png)

## Required Information

- The technician must have been at the customer location within the last 24 hours.
