# Technician Documentation
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

What good technician documentation looks like. Use it to check a job before you tag it Ready to Review.

## Invoice summary notes

- Notes are detailed, complete, and stored in the job's Invoice Summary.
- Notes are signed with first initial, last name, and date. For example, JSmith 3/13/26.
- Notes say the work is done, in words like "job complete" or "no return visit needed."
- Notes are professional and free of spelling and grammar errors.

## Scheduled appointments

- Technicians start the appointment in the app as soon as they arrive on site.
- Technicians complete a JST Service Safety Form at check-in.
- Technicians complete the appointment as soon as they leave the site.
- Technicians update appointment notes within 24 hours.
- Technicians submit their time within 24 hours.

## Job history and files

- Jobsite pictures are uploaded to the job History.
- All required forms are completed on time.
- PCard Receipt Uploads are submitted through the Forms tab.
- PCard Receipt Uploads have verified costs and clear receipt pictures.
