# Supervisor Dashboard Overview
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

How jobs move through the Supervisor Dashboard, from Complete to billed.

## Steps

1. The technician finishes the last appointment and notes that the job is complete. The job moves to the Complete container.
2. You work through the [Job Completion Checklist](job-completion-checklist), then tag the job Ready to Review. It moves to the Ready to Review container.
3. The Field Supervisor reviews the whole job.
4. If something's missing, the Field Supervisor pins a question in History and removes the Ready to Review tag. You track down the missing information with the technician, then tag Ready to Review again.
5. When everything checks out, the Field Supervisor tags the job Ready to Bill. It moves to the Ready to Bill container.
6. Accounting does a final review and bills the job.
