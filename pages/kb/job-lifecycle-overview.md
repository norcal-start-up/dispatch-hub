# Job Lifecycle Overview
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

The big picture: where requests come from, what happens once one lands, and who owns each step until the job is billed.

## Where requests come from

Requests come in one of five ways:

- The customer emails or calls Dispatch directly
- The customer contacts a technician directly
- The customer contacts a sales rep
- Direct Line, the overnight answering service
- Direct Supply, the dispatching service for senior living facilities

If the request comes to you by phone or email, capture the key details on the [Intake Form](intake-form).

## Steps

1. Respond to the customer within 15 minutes.
2. Check the dispatch board for technician availability.
3. Create a job, assign a technician, and schedule the appointment.
4. Email a confirmation to the customer, the technician, and the Field Supervisor.
5. Create POs and coordinate parts and materials.
6. Confirm the technician's notes from the site visit are complete.
7. Approve technician timecards and tag the job Ready to Review.
8. The Field Supervisor tags it Ready to Bill once approved.

## Workflow overview

Each job type starts differently, but they all end with you preparing the job and tagging it Ready to Review.
