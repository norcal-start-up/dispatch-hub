# Direct Supply
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

For requests from Direct Supply, the dispatching service for senior living facilities.

## Steps

1. Once a bid is approved, you'll get an email with a deadline to Assign a Technician and Set an ETA in the Direct Supply portal.
2. Confirm technician availability, then confirm timing with the customer.
3. Once approved, add the technician's name, phone number, and ETA to the request in the portal.
4. Share the site contact information with the technician. Have them call Direct Supply to check in and out of the job: 888-225-7729.
5. Open a job in ServiceTitan. Use the Authorization Number as the Customer PO.
6. Track the job to completion as normal.

![The Reminder to Check In email, and where to find the Authorization Number and the Set ETA and Assign Tech buttons in the portal](direct-supply-01.png)

## Common Errors

- A technician forgets to check in or out with Direct Supply. Direct Supply will email asking for an update. Confirm completion with the technician, then reply with their visit notes so Direct Supply can update the job on their end.
- Responding to requests for bids or quotes. That's a sales function. You only respond to Reminder to Check In and Set ETA emails.
