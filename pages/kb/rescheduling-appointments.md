# Rescheduling Appointments
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a customer or technician needs to move an appointment.

## The policy

- Rescheduling is a last resort.
- An appointment is never rescheduled more than twice.

> Tip: If a third change comes up, loop in the Field Supervisor before you move anything.
