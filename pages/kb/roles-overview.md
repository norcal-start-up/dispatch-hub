# Roles Overview
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Who does what in the field, so you can match the right person to the job.

## Field roles

| Role | What they handle |
| --- | --- |
| Apprentice | Part pickups and smaller repairs while they learn the trade |
| Journeyman | Intermediate diagnostics and repairs, and training apprentices |
| Lead Technician | Complex diagnostics and repairs, and mentoring apprentices |
| Foreman | Supervising work at construction or install sites |
| Field Supervisor | Managing technicians, performance, training, and escalations |

> Note: Names, specialties, and union locals are in the technician roster in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
