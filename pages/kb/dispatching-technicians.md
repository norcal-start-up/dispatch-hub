# Dispatching Technicians
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

How to dispatch, depending on who the request comes from.

## Steps

### When a customer calls or emails

1. Reply to the customer to let them know you received the request. Copy the sales rep and the lead technician.
2. Call the assigned technician to confirm availability. If no one's assigned, check the dispatch board, and clear any new assignment with the Field Supervisor.
3. Confirm the timing works for the customer. Then add the job to the technician's board.
4. Email the customer the agreed date and time the technician will be on site. Copy the sales rep and the assigned technician.

### When a sales rep calls or emails

1. Open a job and create POs, if they asked for them.
2. Reply with the job number, the POs, and the assigned technician.
3. Coordinate with the customer, following the same steps as above.

### When a technician asks for work

1. Find out whether they've already scheduled with the customer, or whether you need to coordinate it.
2. If they found the work during an active visit, they may ask for the technician incentive. See [Applying Technician Incentives](applying-technician-incentives).

### When an apprentice asks for work

1. Call the lead technicians and ask if the apprentice can help, shadow, or pick up parts.
2. If not, call the Field Supervisor to see if they can find the apprentice some work.
3. If the Field Supervisor doesn't answer, work down the escalation contacts in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
