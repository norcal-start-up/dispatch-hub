# Correcting Timecards
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a technician submitted the wrong time, or didn't fill in their time at all.

## Steps

### Find the technician's timesheet

1. Click the profile icon and choose Time Clock.
2. Click View Timesheet.
3. Add the technician's name.
4. Select the right payroll week.

### Add a new entry

1. Click Add Timesheet Entry, then For Individual.
2. Enter the duration, activity type, and job number.
3. Click Add Entry.

### Edit an existing entry

1. Find the entry.
2. Click the edit pencil. It's grayed out if the time was already submitted.
