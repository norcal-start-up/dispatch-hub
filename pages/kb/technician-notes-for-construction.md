# Start-Up Job Notes
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a service technician works on a construction job that lives outside ServiceTitan.

## Steps

1. The technician completes the service call.
2. They submit their time as normal, using the labor transfer cost code.
3. They fill out the Service Request form, or email you their notes.
4. You create a journal entry to copy their notes into the Job Summary.
5. You transfer the cost of their time to the right job.

> Note: The labor transfer cost code is in the Cost Code Reference in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).

## What to send the technician

- Job location
- Location address
- Scope
- Cost code

Use the New Construction Job template in [Email Templates](email-templates).
