# Task Prioritization
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When everything lands at once. Work down this list from the top.

## Steps

### Right away

1. Answer hotline calls and dispatch technicians.
2. Respond to customer emails.
3. Create POs for technicians.
4. Update the dispatch board.

### Next

5. Create POs for account reps.
6. Order parts and materials.
7. Create quoted jobs.
8. Respond to Accounting emails.

### As time allows

9. Respond to vendor emails.
10. Order PM materials.
11. Verify orders received from the warehouse and online vendors.
12. Respond to other Southland email requests.

## Response times

| Who | How fast |
| --- | --- |
| Customers | Within 15 minutes |
| Accounting | Within 24 hours |
