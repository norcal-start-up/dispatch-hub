# Parts Ordering Workflow
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

How parts get ordered from start to finish, and how that drives scheduling.

## How parts get ordered

- Email a parts list or quote to a known supplier contact, with a PO.
- Order online, usually through larger suppliers like Grainger.
- Order over the phone. Rare, but it happens.

## How parts get paid for

- [Purchase order](creating-purchase-orders)
- [PCard](pcard-purchases)

![Parts ordering workflow for T&M, quoted, and PM jobs. Dark green steps are yours.](parts-ordering-workflow-01.png)

## Required Information

- Customer name
- Job number
- Parts list

## Common Errors

- Forgetting to tell the technician the parts have arrived.
- Forgetting to coordinate the part pickup.
- Assuming parts haven't arrived because the warehouse hasn't emailed. Check.
