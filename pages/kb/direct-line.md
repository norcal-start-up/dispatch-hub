# Direct Line
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

For overnight calls answered by Direct Line, Southland's after-hours phone service.

## Steps

1. Review the overnight call notes.
2. Call the technician to find out where things stand.

Where does the call stand?

### If the work is complete

1. Open the job.
2. Capture the technician's notes.
3. Apply the technician's hours.
4. Email the service report to the customer.

### If a technician started but didn't finish

1. Call the technician and schedule the visit.
2. If you can't reach them, call the Field Supervisor to confirm availability.

### If no technician responded

1. Check the dispatch board for availability.
2. Pick the best technician for the job.
3. Clear the assignment with the Field Supervisor.
4. Dispatch as normal.

### In every case

1. Make sure the job is open in ServiceTitan.
2. Email the technician a job confirmation with the job number and any POs. Copy the Field Supervisor.
