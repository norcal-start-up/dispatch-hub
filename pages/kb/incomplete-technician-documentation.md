# Incomplete Notes or Tasking
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

- The customer asked for an update or a service report, and the notes aren't there.
- Notes are the last thing standing between the job and Ready to Review.

## Steps

1. Contact the technician and ask for their job notes.
2. Add the job to their board with the special instruction "Notes please."
3. Email the job number and scope to the technician. Copy the Field Supervisor.
4. Contact the technician at least three times before escalating.
5. Start a group text with the technician and the Field Supervisor. Ask the technician to reply once the notes are done.
6. Call the Field Supervisor and ask for help.

> Tip: When you ask for notes, include the company name, job number, and job description so the technician can find it fast.
