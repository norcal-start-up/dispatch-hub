# Job Tagging
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Tags tell everyone where a job stands at a glance. Here's what each one means.

## Job creation tags

| Tag | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Jobsite | Billable work can be performed at this location |
| Bill To Only | This location is only for billing, not scheduled work |
| Interdepartmental | The work is happening in-house |

## Purchase order tags

| Tag | Means |
| --- | --- |
| PO Sent | POs are open and the job is on the Parts on Order board |
| PO Delayed | Holds the job in Incomplete until the PO is received |
| PO Received | POs are received and ready to bill |

## Job status tags

| Tag | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Ready to Review | The job is complete and ready for Supervisor review |
| Ready to Bill | The Field Supervisor approved the job for billing |
