# Creating New Customers
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a customer has never worked with Southland before. If the customer already exists and just needs a new site, see [Creating New Customer Locations](creating-new-customer-locations).

## Steps

### Northern California customers

1. Send the customer the New Customer email from [Email Templates](email-templates) and collect their W9 and New Customer Data Sheet.
2. Email both to the corporate accounting mailbox to set up the account in COINS.
3. Then email the same documents to the Accounts Receivable mailbox for ServiceTitan setup.

### Internal locations

1. Email the location name, address, and a screenshot of the ServiceTitan account to the Accounts Receivable mailbox.

### Southern California customers

1. Email SoCal Service Dispatch and copy their coordinator.

The mailboxes and contacts are in the Internal Support Directory in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).

## Required Information

- Customer W9
- New Customer Data Sheet
- Company address
- Customer ID
- Screenshots of the account
