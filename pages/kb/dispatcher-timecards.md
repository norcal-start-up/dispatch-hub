# Dispatcher Timecards
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

Your own weekly timesheet. It's due before 10 AM on Friday.

## Steps

1. Open the weekly timesheet workbook. The link is in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
2. Find the sheet tab with your name on it.
3. Fill in your times. Blue cells are prefilled, so adjust them as needed.
4. Check that the Hours Worked column adds up to 8.00 hours a day.
5. Add any overtime, sick or personal time, or vacation. HR adds holiday hours for you.
6. Date it next to your name at the bottom.
7. Email a screenshot to your supervisor.
