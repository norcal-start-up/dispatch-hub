# Assigning Technicians to Appointments
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

To put a technician on an appointment in ServiceTitan.

## Steps

### Option 1

1. Under Scheduled Appointments, click Assign.
2. Type the technician's name and select it.

### Option 2

1. Click the pencil next to Assign.
2. Type the technician's name in the Technicians field.

![Option 1: click Assign and type the name](assigning-technicians-to-appointments-01.png)
![Option 2: the pencil opens Edit Appointment](assigning-technicians-to-appointments-02.png)

## Required Information

- Technician name
- Job number
- Appointment date
