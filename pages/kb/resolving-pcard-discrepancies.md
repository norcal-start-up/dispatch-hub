# Resolving PCard Discrepancies
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a PCard Receipt Upload doesn't match the Materials lines on the invoice.

## Steps

1. Scroll down to History and click the Files tab.
2. Scroll to the PCard Receipt Upload forms and click View.
3. Go to the Invoice Details page.
4. Confirm the PCard lines under Materials match the upload.
5. Fix whichever is wrong with the Edit pencil.
