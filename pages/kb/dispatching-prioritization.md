# Dispatching Prioritization
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

How to pick the right technician for a call.

## Steps

1. Check previous jobs to find the customer's usual technician.
2. Check the dispatch board for lead technician availability.
3. If the usual technician isn't available, find the next journeyman.
4. If no one's available, look at where technicians are and what the scope of work is.
5. Call a technician to try to arrange a call or visit with the customer.
6. If that doesn't work, bring in the Field Supervisor to look at other options:
   - Send an apprentice to put eyes on the issue and collect details.
   - Pull a technician off lower-priority work, if the issue is truly severe.
   - Schedule a visit with the customer for a later date.
