# Resolving Incomplete JST Safety Forms
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

When a job's JST Service Safety Form isn't marked Completed.

## Steps

1. Scroll down to History and click the Files tab.
2. Scroll to the JST Service Safety Form.
3. If it doesn't say Completed, add an appointment with a special instruction: "Please complete your JST Safety Form."
