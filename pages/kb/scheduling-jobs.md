# Scheduling Jobs
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Once a job is created, here's the order to schedule it in.

## Steps

1. Order the parts and get an ETA.
2. Once the parts arrive, confirm technician availability.
3. Agree on a date and time with the customer.
4. Email a scheduling confirmation. Copy the technicians, the sales rep, and the Field Supervisor.
5. Update the dispatch board.

## Required Information

- Part tracking or ETA
- Technician availability
- The customer's available dates and times
