# Job Creation Overview
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Before you open a job, know which of the four types it is. Each one has its own rules.

## The four job types

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Quoted | A sales rep sold a bid with a clear scope and cost. |
| Time and Material (T&M) | The customer pays for technician time and materials to diagnose and fix an unexpected problem. |
| Preventive Maintenance | Routine maintenance at set intervals, under a service agreement. |
| Interdepartmental | Internal jobs for Southland facilities or construction sites. |

## What each type needs

### Quoted

- Attach the quote to the job.
- POs can be received with a parts quote.
- Rate sheet: No Rate Sheets

### Time and Material

- POs must be received with a parts invoice.
- Charge a Service Vehicle Fee for each appointment.

### Preventive Maintenance

- Notes must include a detailed description of the work.
- Send a complete service report after the work is done.

### Interdepartmental

- Tag the job Interdepartmental so it bills correctly.
- Include the construction job number and cost code.
