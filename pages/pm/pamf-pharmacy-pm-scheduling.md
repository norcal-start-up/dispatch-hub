# PAMF Pharmacy PM Scheduling
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

PAMF pharmacy PMs follow their own schedule. The sites, contacts, agreement numbers, and access windows are in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).

## Why it works this way

PAMF's systems reset on the 2nd of every month. To avoid conflicts, half the work happens before the reset and half after.

## The booking structure

- The pharmacy sites are grouped into two location pairs, Pair A and Pair B.
- Each pair is always scheduled together.
- Each site gets two visits:
  - Visit 1: before the 2nd of the month
  - Visit 2: exactly 7 days later, on the same day of the week
- Only book inside the pharmacy access windows listed in the Onboarding Folder.

### Example for March PMs

| Pair | Visit 1 | Visit 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pair A | Tuesday, Feb 24 | Tuesday, Mar 3 |
| Pair B | Wednesday, Feb 25 | Wednesday, Mar 4 |

## Steps

1. Confirm the PM parts and materials are available.
2. Identify the location pair.
3. Choose dates for the pair with the technician. Visit 1 lands before the 2nd, and Visit 2 is 7 days later.
4. Fill in the PM Scheduling template from [Email Templates](email-templates) with the proposed dates.
5. Address it to the site contacts. Copy the account manager and the assigned technician.
6. Confirm availability, and reschedule if needed.
7. Book the appointment on the dispatch board.
8. Track the job through completion, and make sure all notes and tasking are done.
9. Prepare the service report and email it to the customer within 24 hours of the PM.
