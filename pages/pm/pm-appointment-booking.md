# PM Appointment Booking
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

To book a PM visit from the customer's service agreement.

## Steps

1. Search for the customer location.
2. Scroll down to Service Agreements.
3. Click the Agreement ID.
4. Find the upcoming visit under Remaining Visits.
5. Click the visit name to see the scope.
6. Click Book Job, under the Job # column.
7. Schedule as normal.

![Service Agreements, then Remaining Visits. Click the visit name for scope, or Book Job to schedule. Customer details are blurred.](pm-appointment-booking-01.png)

> Note: Visit windows in red are past due. Book those first.
