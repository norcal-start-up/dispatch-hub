# PM Part Ordering
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

To order parts for upcoming PM visits. Every PM customer has a premade parts list.

## Steps

1. Open the NorCal Service SharePoint site. The link is in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
2. Open the Dispatch folder.
3. Open PM Material Lists, then the current year's parts lists.
4. Find the customer's file and follow its instructions.

> Tip: The [PM Tracking Workbook](pm-tracking-workbook) can draft every part order email for the month at once.

## Required Information

- Customer name

## Common Errors

- Not telling technicians when parts are on order or ready for pickup.
