# PM Service Report Compilation
Platform: ServiceTrade
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

After all the PM work is done, to send the customer their service report.

## Steps

1. Confirm all appointments are completed.
2. Confirm the technician's notes are detailed and complete.
3. Confirm the job paperwork is completed and uploaded.
4. Go to Actions, then Send Service Link. Make these visible:
   - Job Description
   - Job Services
   - Comments
   - Attachments
   - Deficiencies
5. Keep Parts, Labor, and Items Used hidden.
6. Toggle on every contact who should get the link.
7. Under Message Includes Appointment Details, choose Do not include appointment details.
8. Send it, then mark the job complete.

## Common Errors

- Leaving Parts, Labor, or Items Used visible. Customers shouldn't see internal costs.
- Including appointment details in the message.
