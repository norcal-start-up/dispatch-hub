# PM Workflow Checklist
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

Every stage of a PM visit, from planning to Ready to Bill. Tick it off for each visit.

## How a PM moves

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Review upcoming visits | You review service agreements and find which visits are due. |
| Coordinate parts | Parts are ordered and received, with POs as needed. |
| Schedule the visit | You contact the customer and schedule the technician. |
| Perform the maintenance | The technician completes the inspection, notes, photos, and tasking forms. |
| Compile documentation | You check the notes, forms, and attachments. |
| Service reporting | You compile the report and send it to the customer. |
| Closeout | Time is verified, POs applied, and the job tagged Ready to Review. |
| Supervisor review | The Field Supervisor tags it Ready to Bill. |

## PM planning

- [ ] Upcoming PM visits and lead technicians are identified
- [ ] PM appointments are created in ServiceTitan

## Parts and materials

- [ ] POs are created and issued
- [ ] Orders are placed by email, phone, or vendor portal
- [ ] Shipment tracking is confirmed and shared with the technician
- [ ] Parts are received and coordinated: will call, hub delivery, or technician pickup
- [ ] Invoices are received in ServiceTitan

## Schedule the visit

- [ ] The visit is scheduled
- [ ] The customer confirmation email is sent
- [ ] Any rescheduling or cancellation is coordinated

## Technician documentation

- [ ] The PM inspection scope is shared with the technician
- [ ] Technician notes are complete
- [ ] All tasking forms are complete
- [ ] Photos and attachments are in the job

## Service reporting

- [ ] Documentation is compiled into a service report
- [ ] The report meets formatting standards
- [ ] The report is sent to the customer

## Billing readiness and closeout

- [ ] Time is verified
- [ ] All POs are received
- [ ] The job is tagged Ready to Review

## Common Errors

Watch for these exceptions and escalate them:

- Additional work found during the PM
- A return visit is needed
- Parts on back order or unavailable
- Warranty issues
