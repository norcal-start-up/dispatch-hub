# PM Overview
Platform: ServiceTitan
Status: Active

## When This Is Used

The planning rhythm for preventive maintenance visits under service agreements.

## Steps

1. Review every PM coming up this month.
2. Add any unfinished work from earlier months.
3. Create POs and order parts for each customer.
4. Use the part ETAs to start scheduling with customers.
5. Put confirmed visits on the dispatch board.
6. Coordinate part pickups with each technician.
7. Track the job as normal through completion.
8. Confirm the job notes, forms, and tasking are complete.
9. Compile the notes, forms, and tasking into a service report.
10. Send the service report to the customer and tag the job Ready to Review.
