# Home

## How this hub works

New here? Open Start Here and work through Your First Month. Each week links straight to the articles you need.

Looking for one task? Use the search bar at the top, or open the Knowledge Base. Every article follows the same pattern: when to use it, the steps, what you need, and the mistakes to avoid.

Anything with names, phone numbers, account numbers, or codes lives in SharePoint, not here. Those links show a lock and need your Southland login.

> Tip: Press the slash key on your keyboard to jump to search from anywhere.

## What's active and what's archived

Active articles describe how the work is done today in ServiceTitan. Archived articles cover the retired ServiceTrade and COINS workflows. They live on the Archive page as context for older jobs, and they are no longer maintained. Search shows active pages first.
