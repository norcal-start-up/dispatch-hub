# Refrigerant Reports
Platform: ServiceTrade
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

To pull a customer's refrigerant records for the previous year from ServiceTrade.

## Steps

1. Go to All Jobs and select the Job Items tab.
2. Service Line: Refrigerant
3. Job Customer: the customer
4. Open Advanced Filters. Set Created From to January 1 of last year and Created To to December 31 of last year.
5. Download the report.
6. Open each job and scroll down to Attachments for the details.
