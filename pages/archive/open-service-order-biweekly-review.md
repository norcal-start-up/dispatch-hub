# Open Service Order Biweekly Review
Platform: COINS
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

The second and fourth Friday of each month, when dispatch and sales reps met to review open work.

## What was reviewed

- Every open service order from the previous month
- Any service order with more than $5,000 in costs
- PM agreement statuses, to track expirations and renewals

> Tip: Notes had to be clear, complete, and current well before the meeting.
