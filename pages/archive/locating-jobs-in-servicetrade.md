# Locating Jobs in ServiceTrade
Platform: ServiceTrade
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

To find a legacy job in ServiceTrade.

## Steps

### By ServiceTrade job number

1. Hover over the truck in the left sidebar and choose All Jobs.
2. Type the number into Job Number.

### By COINS service order number

1. Hover over the truck and choose All Jobs.
2. Type the service order number into Tag.

### By location name or address

1. Type the location name or address into the search bar at the top of any page.
2. Select the location.
3. Look through the job descriptions, or search them in the Filter Jobs bar.

## Required Information

- Job number, service order number, or location
- Scope of work
