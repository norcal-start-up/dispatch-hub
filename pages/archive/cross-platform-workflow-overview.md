# Cross-Platform Workflow Overview
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

The timeline for moving operations from ServiceTrade and COINS to ServiceTitan.

## Phase 1: Partial migration, months 0 to 1

Dispatch, Billing, and Sales review job statuses, estimated time to completion, renewal schedules, and billing considerations to build the Phase 1 transition list.

### Close out in ServiceTrade

- Jobs with significant costs already hitting
- Jobs that will close within two weeks
- PM agreements that won't renew
- PM agreements that renew within three months

### Move to ServiceTitan

- New jobs and service agreements
- Jobs not started in ServiceTrade yet
- Jobs without significant costs hitting yet
- Jobs started in the last two weeks that won't close for 2 or more weeks

## Phase 2: New process adoption, months 1 to 2

- Dispatch settles into a normal rhythm in ServiceTitan.
- Technicians work through the new notes and time processes.
- Every team works to close out ServiceTrade jobs as quickly as possible.

## Phase 3: Complete migration and closeout, months 3 to 4

1. Dispatch lists every remaining ServiceTrade job.
2. ServiceTrade jobs get scheduling priority so they finish faster.
3. All ServiceTrade notes are transferred and all jobs closed by the end of April.
4. ServiceTrade documentation is archived.
5. COINS documentation is reviewed and archived where it's no longer needed.
6. Knowledge base links point to ServiceTitan documentation from May 1.
7. COINS documentation stays where exception workflows still exist.
