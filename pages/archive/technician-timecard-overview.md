# Technician Timecard Overview
Platform: ServiceTrade
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

The legacy weekly timecard review in ServiceTrade.

## Steps

1. Select each technician to review their hours.
2. Confirm the hours follow the rules below.
3. Check the jobs to confirm the time makes sense.
4. Contact the technician about any errors or questions.
5. Approve all hours before 9 AM Monday. If Monday is a holiday, approve before 10 AM Friday.
6. Export all time and run it through the [submission steps](submitting-technician-timecards).

## The rules

- Monday through Friday, the first 8 hours are Straight Time.
- Overtime only starts after 8 Straight Time hours in a day.
- Time before 6 AM, after 2 PM, or on Saturday is Overtime.
- Time on Sunday is Double Time.

## Deadlines

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| All timecards submitted, technicians and yours | 10 AM Friday. Weekend workers can submit early Monday. |
| Technician timecards approved and sent to payroll | 9 AM Monday. 10 AM Friday if Monday is a holiday. |
