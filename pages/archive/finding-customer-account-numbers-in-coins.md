# Finding Customer Account Numbers in COINS
Platform: COINS
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

To look up a legacy customer account number.

## Steps

1. In the left menu, open Accounts Receivable, then Customers, then Customers.
2. In the bottom right, change the search type as needed.

![Accounts Receivable, Customers. Account details are blurred.](finding-customer-account-numbers-in-coins-01.png)
![Change the search type in the bottom right](finding-customer-account-numbers-in-coins-02.png)

## Required Information

- Customer name
- Address

## Common Errors

- Not finding the customer by name. Try an asterisk before and after the name.
- Still nothing. Search by address.
- Still nothing. Check with a teammate. The customer may have an unusual spelling, or may need to be set up as new.
