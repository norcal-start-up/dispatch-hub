# PM Assignment and Tasking for PAMF
Platform: COINS
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

To put PAMF PM appointments and tasking on technician boards in COINS.

## Steps

1. In the left menu, go to Service Management, Scheduling, then Schedule and Dispatch.
2. Type the COINS service order number into the search box in the bottom right.
3. Drag the service order onto the right day on the technician's calendar.
