# COINS Workflow Overview
Platform: COINS
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

COINS is the old system. After the migration, dispatchers used it for only three things.

## What COINS is still used for

- Creating truck stock purchase orders
- Looking up legacy customer and supplier information
- PM assignment and tasking for PAMF accounts

Everything else happens in ServiceTitan.
