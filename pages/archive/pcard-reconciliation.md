# PCard Reconciliation
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

The legacy monthly PCard reconciliation in the bank portal.

## Steps

1. Click Mass Allocate at the bottom of the page.
2. Choose the GL codes for office or field purchases. The codes are in the Cost Code Reference in the [Onboarding Folder](onboarding-folder).
3. Upload the receipts.
4. Copy each charge's description into the sign-off. "Parts for job" is fine.
