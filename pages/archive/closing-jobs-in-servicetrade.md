# Closing Jobs in ServiceTrade
Platform: ServiceTrade
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

To close a legacy job in ServiceTrade.

## Steps

1. Find the job. See [Locating Jobs in ServiceTrade](locating-jobs-in-servicetrade).
2. Confirm every item on the completion checklist is done.
3. Open the Actions dropdown in the top right.
4. Click Complete Job.
