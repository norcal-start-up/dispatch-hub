# Creating New Locations in COINS
Platform: COINS
Status: Archived

## When This Is Used

When a legacy construction request came in for a location that wasn't in COINS.

## Steps

### Rule out a duplicate first

1. In Service Management, Locations, search every variation of the location name and the address.
2. Check with other coordinators for unusual naming.
3. As a last resort, ask the technician whether we've serviced the site before.

### Create the location

1. In Service Management, Locations, click Add.
2. Location Name: customer, city, and street number. For example, Example Health Milpitas 100.
3. Fill in the address, city, state, ZIP, and geographic area.
4. Technician Coverage: NORCAL. Service Manager: from the Onboarding Folder.
5. PR tab: State CA. Work Comp State CA.
6. Analysis tab: Region 56, Northern California.
7. Save, then search for the location to confirm.

### Add the customer to the location

A location is just a site. It needs a customer to be complete.

1. Select the new location, choose Add to This Company, and open the location.
2. On the Customers tab, click Add and fill in the customer account number.
3. Invoice Consolidation: Separate. Bill To Address Source: Customer. Require PO: Not Required.
4. Response types: NRML. Department and group codes: from the Onboarding Folder.
5. Tax codes: the county's taxable code, and CAEX for exempt. Check Material, and Equipment where it applies.
6. Save, undo, and confirm the customer account column has a number.
7. Open the customer account, add the non-agreement allowable work line, and fill in the state fields.
