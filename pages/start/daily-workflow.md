# Daily Workflow

What a normal day looks like, and who owns each step on the way to billing.

## Your daily rhythm

1. Review and respond to email requests.
2. Review completed jobs on the [Supervisor Dashboard](supervisor-dashboard-overview).
3. Identify discrepancies or missing information.
4. Schedule appointments if a job is incomplete.
5. Tag Ready to Review once every job item is complete.
6. Fill the dispatch board with jobs for the next day.

On Fridays, also send the morning On Call email. The template is in [Email Templates](email-templates).

## What happens after you

Once you tag a job Ready to Review, it leaves your hands:

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Field Supervisor | Reviews job notes, forms, and costs. Flags anything missing. Tags Ready to Bill when everything checks out. |
| Billing | Confirms all billing information. Sends the invoice to the customer. The job is closed and billed. |

## The daily status email

A short morning email to technicians keeps jobs moving. List the jobs that are missing notes, and the jobs that need return visits, so techs can update you without being chased one by one.
