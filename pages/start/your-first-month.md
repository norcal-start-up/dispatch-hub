# Your First Month

This is a bird's-eye view of your first four weeks, not a strict schedule. You may pick things up faster or slower than what's laid out here, and that's fine. Use it to see where you're headed.

## By the end of your first month

You should be able to do all of this on your own:

- Create and track quoted, T&M, and PM jobs
- Process all material and truck stock PO requests
- Coordinate parts, equipment, and subcontractors
- Communicate scheduling details clearly to everyone involved
- Identify and resolve obstacles to job completion and billing
- Triage urgent requests internally and coordinate timely coverage

## Week 1: Getting Started

- [ ] Review the [Onboarding Checklist](onboarding-checklist) with your trainer
- [ ] Set up your system links, logins, and contacts
- [ ] Meet the technicians, in person or by phone
- [ ] Explore the [Job Lifecycle Overview](job-lifecycle-overview)
- [ ] Learn the difference between quoted, T&M, and PM jobs in the [Job Creation Overview](job-creation-overview)
- [ ] Review incoming emails and pick out the action items
- [ ] Shadow daily dispatch conversations and core functions
- [ ] Practice opening jobs and purchase orders with oversight

## Week 2: Hands-On Learning

- [ ] Open jobs and purchase orders on your own
- [ ] Start sending the Friday morning On Call email (see [Email Templates](email-templates))
- [ ] Practice [Receiving Purchase Orders](receiving-purchase-orders) and tagging jobs Ready to Review
- [ ] Join the daily review of the [Supervisor Dashboard](supervisor-dashboard-overview)
- [ ] Start making outbound calls to collect information from technicians
- [ ] Start the daily review of timecards, notes, and the [Job Completion Checklist](job-completion-checklist)

## Week 3: The Dispatch Board

- [ ] Shadow daily and weekly dispatch board scheduling
- [ ] Practice filling and adjusting jobs on the dispatch board
- [ ] Explore the [PM Overview](pm-overview) and PM workflows
- [ ] Start helping with inbound technician and customer calls
- [ ] Help coordinate parts and customer scheduling

## Week 4: Full-Scale Integration

- [ ] Start daily and weekly dispatch board scheduling
- [ ] Coordinate Preventive Maintenance parts and service
- [ ] Expedite job completion and billing, especially at month's end
- [ ] [Test your knowledge](check-yourself)
