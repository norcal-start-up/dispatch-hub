# Test Your Knowledge

Ten quick questions to see what's sticking. Pick an answer and you'll see why it's right or wrong. Nothing is recorded.

### You get to the office and your inbox is full. A sales account manager (SAM) wants a new job opened, a technician wants a job on their board, a customer has requested service, a parts order email bounced, and Accounting needs a PO "ASAP." What do you answer first?

- [ ] The SAM's new job
- [ ] Accounting's PO
- [x] The customer's service request
- [ ] The bounced parts order email

> Why: Customers come first, with a reply inside 15 minutes. Accounting gets a 24-hour response window. See [Task Prioritization](task-prioritization).

### A SAM emails a quote to a customer and copies NorCal Service. What do you do?

- [ ] Create the job right away
- [ ] Reply to the customer to confirm
- [ ] Ask the sales rep clarifying questions
- [x] Nothing yet

> Why: Wait for the customer to approve the quote before creating a job. Quoted work starts when the estimate is marked Sold. See [Creating Quoted Jobs](creating-quoted-jobs).

### A SAM asks you to set up a new location for a customer. What do you do first?

- [ ] Start setting up the location
- [x] Search for the existing customer name and account number

> Why: Most "new" locations belong to an existing customer. Searching first avoids duplicates. See [Creating New Customer Locations](creating-new-customer-locations).

### The warehouse emails that parts have arrived. What's your next move?

- [ ] Receive the PO
- [x] Let the technician know and coordinate pickup and scheduling
- [ ] Wait for the technician to ask

> Why: The most common miss in parts ordering is forgetting to tell the technician. See [Parts Ordering Workflow](parts-ordering-workflow).

### Which rate sheet does a quoted job use?

- [ ] NorCal Contract
- [ ] NorCal Non-Contract
- [x] No Rate Sheets

> Why: The price was already set in the quote. T&M jobs use NorCal Contract or NorCal Non-Contract. See [Job Completion Checklist](job-completion-checklist).

### Who tags a job Ready to Bill?

- [ ] You, the dispatcher
- [x] The Field Supervisor
- [ ] Accounting

> Why: You tag Ready to Review. The Field Supervisor reviews the job and tags Ready to Bill. See [Supervisor Dashboard Overview](supervisor-dashboard-overview).

### Direct Supply emails asking for a bid on a repair. What do you do?

- [ ] Send a quote
- [ ] Forward it to the nearest technician
- [x] Leave it for Sales

> Why: Bids and quotes are a sales function. You only respond to "Set ETA" and "Reminder to Check In" emails. See [Direct Supply](direct-supply).

### A PO request mentions "testing and balancing." Which line type is it most likely?

- [ ] Material
- [x] Subcontractor
- [ ] Freight

> Why: Words like inspecting, repair work, engineering services, and testing and balancing point to subcontractor work. See [Material or Subcontractor PO?](differentiating-po-requests).

### You need to change a PO, but the Edit button is grayed out. What now?

- [ ] Ask the technician to edit it
- [x] Create a new PO, because Accounts Payable already processed it
- [ ] Wait a day and try again

> Why: You can edit until AP processes the PO. After that, make a new one. See [Editing POs After Receiving](editing-pos-after-receiving).

### A customer wants to move their appointment for the third time. What's the policy?

- [ ] Reschedule as many times as needed
- [x] Appointments aren't rescheduled more than twice, so loop in the Field Supervisor
- [ ] Cancel the job

> Why: Rescheduling is a last resort, and never more than twice. See [Rescheduling Appointments](rescheduling-appointments).
